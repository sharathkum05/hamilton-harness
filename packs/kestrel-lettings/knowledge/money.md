# Deposits and fees

## Application fee
There is no fee to apply or to view a home.

## Holding deposit
Once an application is accepted, a holding deposit of ₹5,000 takes the home off
the market. It comes off your first month's rent. The lettings team sends the
payment details. Payment is never taken in this chat.

## Security deposit
The security deposit is 2 months' rent, paid before you move in and returned at
the end of the tenancy less any agreed deductions.

## Tenancy length
The first tenancy is for 11 months. Either side gives 2 months' notice after
that.
