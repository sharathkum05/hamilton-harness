# Applying for a home

## How to apply
You can apply here in the chat. We take the home you want, your move-in date,
how many people will live there, your name, email, phone and work situation.

## Documents
After you apply the lettings team emails a secure link to upload documents:
photo ID, your last 3 payslips or 6 months of bank statements, and a reference
from your current landlord if you have one. Never send documents in this chat.

## Who decides
The lettings team and the owner decide on every application. They reply within
2 working days of receiving your documents. Nobody in this chat can approve or
turn down an application.

## More than one applicant
Everyone aged 18 or over who will live in the home is named on the application
and sends their own documents.
