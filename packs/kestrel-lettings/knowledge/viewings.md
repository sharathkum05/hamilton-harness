# Viewings

## When viewings happen
Viewings run Monday to Saturday, between 10 am and 6 pm. A viewing takes about
20 minutes. There are no viewings on Sundays.

## Booking a viewing
Take a viewing request with the home, a preferred day and time, a name and a
phone number. The lettings team calls back to confirm the time.

## On the day
Someone from the lettings team meets you at the home. Bring photo ID.
