# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days across India. Express delivery
takes 1 to 2 working days and is available in metro cities.

## Delivery charges
Standard delivery is free on orders above ₹1,999. Below that it costs ₹99.
Express delivery costs ₹199 on every order.

## Tracking
Every order gets a tracking link by text message once it leaves the warehouse.
The rep can resend the link at any time.

## Late orders
An order is late when it has not arrived two working days after the promised
date. Late orders can have the delivery charge refunded.
