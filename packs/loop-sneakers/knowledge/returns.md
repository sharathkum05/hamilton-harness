# Returns and exchanges

## Return window
Shoes can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unworn and in the
original box. Sale items marked Final Sale cannot be returned.

## Exchanges
Size exchanges are free within 30 days of delivery. The new pair ships once the
exchange is created, and a prepaid return label is emailed to the customer.

## Refund timing
Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 3 to 5 working days to
show up.

## Damaged or wrong items
If a pair arrives damaged or is the wrong item, the customer gets a full refund
or a replacement, whichever they prefer. A photo helps but is not required.
