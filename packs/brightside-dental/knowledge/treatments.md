# Treatments and prices

## Check-up and clean
A check-up with a scale and polish costs ₹1,200 and takes 45 minutes. New
patients get X-rays at no extra charge on their first visit.

## Fillings
A tooth-coloured filling starts from ₹2,500. The final price depends on the
size of the filling and is confirmed by the dentist before any work begins.

## Root canal
Root canal treatment starts from ₹8,000 and usually needs two visits. It is
booked by the dentist after a check-up, not at the front desk.

## Whitening
Professional whitening costs ₹12,000 for the full treatment, which is one
visit of 90 minutes.

## Braces and aligners
A braces or aligner consultation is free and takes 30 minutes. Treatment
prices are given at the consultation.
