# Visiting us

## Opening hours
The practice is open Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 7 pm. It is closed on Sundays
and public holidays.

## Finding us
Brightside Dental is on the first floor of the Lakeview Arcade. There is a lift.
Parking in the arcade is free for the first 2 hours.

## Before your appointment
Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit so we can set up your
record. Bring a list of any medicines you take.

## Cancelling or moving an appointment
You can move or cancel an appointment free of charge up to 24 hours before it.
Inside 24 hours there is a ₹500 late cancellation fee.
