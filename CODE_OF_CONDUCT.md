# Code of conduct

This project is a place to build software together. Everyone who takes part,
in issues, pull requests and discussions, is expected to make that easy for
everyone else.

## What that means

- Be kind and assume good faith. Most confusing messages are just short ones.
- Criticise the code or the idea, never the person.
- Make room for people who are new, to the project or to programming.
- Accept that maintainers may say no to a change, and that it is not personal.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour about anyone's race, ethnicity,
  nationality, caste, religion, gender, sexuality, age, disability or
  appearance.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Continuing to do any of this after being asked to stop.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests,
and block people from the repository, with or without a warning, depending on
how serious it is.

To report something, contact the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are kept
private.
